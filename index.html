Our Story

A Place to
Rest, Meet & Thrive

Lelesha Lodge & Apartments is Lusaka's warm answer to modern hospitality. Tucked on Chimwala Road, we offer a seamless blend of comfortable lodge rooms, self-catering apartments, a sparkling pool, and fully equipped conference facilities — all under one roof.

Whether you're a business traveller, a family on a city break, or a corporate team looking for a focused offsite venue — Lelesha is built around your comfort.
